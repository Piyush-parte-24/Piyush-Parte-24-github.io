🏛️ Presidents & Prime Ministers of India — From the Raj's Twilight to the Republic's Dawn 🗽

✒️ When the Union Jack lowered on 15 August 1947, and the Tricolour rose over the Red Fort, a new chapter began. The President is the ceremonial Head of State — the constitutional heir to the erstwhile Crown — while the Prime Minister is the Head of Government, wielding real executive power. Two offices, one grand republic. 🎖️

🔎 Insight: The President is chosen not by the public but by an Electoral College of elected MPs and MLAs. Though largely ceremonial, the office holds a "nuclear button" of powers — the pocket veto, the power to return bills, and the crucial discretion during a hung Parliament. Zail Singh's famous pocket veto of the Postal Bill (1986) proved the office is never truly toothless. 🦷

First Prime Minister

Jawaharlal Nehru (1947–1964) — served 16 years, 286 days.

Dr. Rajendra Prasad — also the only 2-term President.

🌿

Current President

Droupadi Murmu — 15th & first tribal woman President, since 25 Jul 2022.

Narendra Modi — since 26 May 2014; 3rd consecutive term from 9 Jun 2024.

Only Acting PM

Gulzarilal Nanda served twice as caretaker (1964 & 1966).
