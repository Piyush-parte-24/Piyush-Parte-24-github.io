🧭 Act I · The Constitutional Map

Merit is the purpose. Independence is the method.

The Union Public Service Commission is an independent constitutional body. Its core work is impartial selection and expert advice on public services. It is not the government's general personnel department, not a court, and not the appointing authority for the posts it recruits to.

📖XIV

Part XIV · “Services under the Union and the States”

Chapter I (Arts. 308–314) deals with the services. Chapter II (Arts. 315–323) deals with Public Service Commissions.

🏛️315

The constitutional anchor

Provides for a Union PSC, a PSC for each State, a Joint State PSC by agreement, and UPSC service to a State on request.

🕰️1926

Origins

First standing PSC: 1 October 1926. Constitutional UPSC from 26 January 1950, with continuity under Article 378.

📍DELHI

🛡️ Why constitutional status matters

The Commission's existence, core functions and safeguards come from the Constitution, not an executive resolution. Governments must work within that framework.

Yet constitutional status is not unlimited power: the Commission is not immune from judicial review, from audit, or from the law.

🐕‍🦺 “Watchdog of the merit system”

A popular textbook phrase — not constitutional text and not a grant of general supervisory power. Merit operates alongside lawful reservation and equal opportunity.

🚨 Three distinctions: selection ≠ appointment (the competent authority appoints) · advice on discipline ≠ imposing a penalty · conducting examinations ≠ managing the whole service afterwards.

🕰️ Act II · The Historical Corridor

From colonial competition to constitutional public service.

Two strands run together: the idea of competitive recruitment and the creation of a standing commission. Do not mix up the commissions that carry similar names.

1854 · MACAULAY COMMITTEE

Recommended open competitive examinations instead of patronage for the civil service. Competitive ICS examinations began in London in 1855.

1886–87 · AITCHISON COMMISSION

The “Public Service Commission” under Sir Charles Aitchison reviewed service classification and Indian participation. It was not the standing PSC of 1926.

1912 · ISLINGTON COMMISSION

The Royal Commission on the Public Services in India. Remembered for proposing roughly 25% of higher posts for Indians.

1919 · GOVERNMENT OF INDIA ACT (s. 96C)

Provided a legal basis for a Public Service Commission. Statutory provision and actual establishment are different events.

1922 · ICS EXAMINATION IN INDIA

The ICS examination began to be held in India as well as in London, widening access within colonial limits.

1923–24 · LEE COMMISSION

The Royal Commission on the Superior Civil Services urged the early establishment of a Public Service Commission. Lee belongs to the 1920s, not 1919.

1 OCTOBER 1926 · FIRST STANDING PSC

Established with Sir Ross Barker as Chairman. Its role was limited and largely advisory.

1935 ACT → 1937 · FEDERAL & PROVINCIAL PSCs

The Government of India Act, 1935 provided for a Federal PSC and Provincial PSCs. The Federal Public Service Commission began functioning in 1937.

26 JANUARY 1950 · UPSC UNDER THE CONSTITUTION

Articles 315–323 came into force; Article 378 continued the Federal PSC as the UPSC. H. K. Kripalani is commonly listed as the first Chairman under the Constitution.

1 NOVEMBER 1956 · MPPSC

The Madhya Pradesh PSC began with the reorganisation of States; headquarters at Indore.

1976 · KOTHARI REPORT & 41ST AMENDMENT

The Kothari Committee proposed a three-stage civil services scheme (Prelims → Mains → Interview), adopted from 1979. The 41st Amendment (1976) raised the State/Joint PSC age ceiling from 60 to 62.

2011 → 2015 · CSAT

CSAT (Paper II) was introduced in 2011 and made qualifying only from 2015. Always confirm current scheme details in the notification.

🧯 Historical repair: Aitchison 1886 · Islington 1912 · Lee 1924. Earlier notes that placed Aitchison in 1935 or Islington in 1886 were wrong.

ARTICLE315🏛️ Establishment & Federal ArchitectureUnion · State · Joint State Commission · UPSC assisting a State
STARTING POINT

There shall be a Public Service Commission for the Union and a Public Service Commission for each State — subject to the arrangements this article permits.

ARTICLE316👥 Appointment, Qualification & TenureAppointing authority · one-half rule · 6 years / 65–62 · no same-office reappointment
THE EXACT EXPRESSION

“As nearly as may be one-half” of the members must, at the date of appointment, have held office for at least ten years under the Government of India or a State Government.

👤 Three arrangements side by side

🎓 What does the Constitution prescribe?

  • No degree, exam or minimum age. The only composition rule is the ten-years-service proviso for about half the members; pre-Constitution service under the Crown or an Indian State counts.
  • It does not prescribe a “50% bureaucrats / 50% outsiders” quota, and it fixes no total number of members — that is set by regulations under Article 318.
  • No oath for PSC members is prescribed in the Third Schedule — do not invent a “constitutional oath by the President or Governor”.

⏳ Clauses to know

  • 316(1A): if the Chairman's office is vacant, or he is absent or unable to act, one of the other members (appointed by the President/Governor) performs the duties. There is no constitutional “six-month maximum” and no “senior-most” rule.
  • 316(2): the term runs from entering office; resignation is in writing to the appropriate authority; removal is only under Article 317.
  • 316(3): a person who has held the office is ineligible for reappointment to that office. “Reappointment is permissible but rare” is wrong. Movement to a different PSC office is governed by Article 319.

ARTICLE317⚖️ Removal & SuspensionPresident removes · Supreme Court inquiry for misbehaviour · Governor may suspend an SPSC member
THE CENTRAL SAFEGUARD

Removal of a Union, State or Joint PSC member is by the President. For misbehaviour, only after the Supreme Court — on a Presidential reference — has inquired and reported that removal is warranted.

317(2)Suspension pending the President's orders on the report.President: UPSC/Joint · Governor: State PSC.
317(3)President may remove, without the 317(1) route, for insolvency, paid employment outside duties, or infirmity of mind/body.“In the President's opinion” applies to infirmity.
317(4)Being concerned or interested in a government contract, or sharing its benefit, is deemed misbehaviour.Exception: being a member, in common with others, of an incorporated company.

ARTICLE320⚙️ Functions, Consultation & ExceptionsExams · joint schemes · advisory heads · exemption regulations
THE OPERATIONAL CORE

Commissions conduct examinations and give expert advice on specified service matters. The advice is recommendatory — but the duty to consult, and the reporting of non-acceptance, are real.

📝 Examples of UPSC processes

Civil Services, Indian Forest Service, Engineering Services, Combined Medical Services, Economic/Statistical Services, CAPF (AC), NDA & NA and CDS written examinations, plus recruitment by selection for notified posts. Each is governed by its own rules and notification.

🌲 IFS ambiguity

The three All India Services are IAS, IPS and the Indian Forest Service. The Indian Foreign Service is a Central service.

💬 Consultation ≠ concurrence

The Supreme Court (Manbodhan Lal Srivastava, 1957) treated the consultation provision as directory in the relevant sense: non-consultation does not by itself automatically invalidate an action. Yet statutory rules, natural justice and the ban on arbitrary action still apply, and Article 323 requires non-acceptance to be explained to the Legislature.

  • Union exemptions: the UPSC (Exemption from Consultation) Regulations, 1958, as amended. States have their own Governor-made regulations — never assume one State's list applies to another.
  • “All appointments under one year are automatically exempt” and “emergencies are an unlimited exemption” are too broad. Check the actual regulation and its conditions.
  • Article 320 is not a rule that every pay, leave or ordinary pension decision must be referred.

ARTICLE323📊 Annual Reports & AccountabilityReport → memorandum on non-acceptance → Legislature

The Commission reports annually. The report is laid before the Legislature together with a memorandum explaining why its advice was not accepted in the relevant cases.

The Constitution prescribes annual reporting, not a universal July–August deadline, a six-month laying deadline, a “three copies” rule, or a mandatory Action Taken Report — committee practice may add follow-up. The memorandum is the government's explanation, not an independent finding of the Commission.

📌 The annual report is not a statutory audit report, and Article 323 does not itself create a general “special report” procedure identical to the annual one.

🎯 Civil Services Examination

Structure: Preliminary → Main → Personality Test. The Preliminary stage is a screening test; final ranking follows the notified scheme. Attempts, age limits, modes and vacancies must be read from the current notice.

🌲 Indian Forest Service

Uses the Civil Services Preliminary as screening under the notified arrangement, then its own Main examination and interview. It is not the Indian Foreign Service route.

💼 Recruitment by selection

Specialised posts may use shortlisting, tests or interviews as stated in the recruitment process. Read the rules and advertisement — it is not a separate clause of Article 320.

🪜 Promotion & service matters

UPSC advises on promotion suitability and takes part in specified processes under rules (for example, promotion from State services to All India Services). Not every State promotion is a UPSC matter.

📌 Selection ≠ guaranteed appointment

🛡️ Act V · The Independence Architecture

🗺️ History, territory & institutional continuity

MPPSC was constituted on 1 November 1956 in the setting of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956. The reorganised State brought together territories from the former Madhya Pradesh, Madhya Bharat, Vindhya Pradesh and Bhopal arrangements. Its headquarters is at Indore. Later territorial reorganisation, including the creation of Chhattisgarh in 2000, must be understood through the relevant reorganisation law rather than by assuming that today’s boundaries existed in 1956.

🏛️ Constitutional permanence needs careful wording: a State executive cannot simply abolish the constitutional PSC framework. But Article 315 itself permits a Joint Commission or a UPSC service arrangement, and reorganisation legislation can make consequential institutional arrangements. So “a State commission can only ever be changed by constitutional amendment” is too absolute.

⚙️ What MPPSC actually does

📝

Competitive examinations

Conducts notified examinations for State services and other posts assigned to it. The State Service Examination is a major route, but MPPSC’s work is not confined to that examination.

Other examinations or selections — such as the State Forest Service or specialist recruitment — must be read through their own advertisements, service rules and current schemes.

🔬

Recruitment by selection

Notified specialist, teaching, medical, technical or other posts may use shortlisting, written tests, interviews or combinations prescribed by the applicable rules.

Do not assume that every post follows the State Service Examination pattern or that an interview is constitutionally compulsory for every recruitment.

💬

Service advice

Advises on recruitment methods, principles of appointment and promotion, suitability, disciplinary matters, legal-defence cost claims and injury-pension claims within Article 320 and the applicable exemption framework.

🌐

Additional statutory functions

Additional responsibilities may be conferred by law under Article 321. A particular university, corporation or local body is covered only where the relevant legal arrangement actually provides for it.

🚨 Not MPPSC’s general job: maintaining every employee’s service book, training the entire State civil service, deciding every transfer, imposing every disciplinary penalty, or controlling every State recruiting agency. These belong to the competent departments and authorities unless a specific law or rule provides otherwise.

⚖️ Reservation, litigation & result interpretation

Reservation operates through the Constitution, applicable legislation, rules and binding judicial orders. A result may contain provisional categories, withheld recommendations or other qualifications arising from ongoing proceedings.

Never convert an interim order or a temporary result formula into a permanent statement of constitutional law. Read the operative order and the notification governing that particular recruitment.

⚖️ “Employment under government” is a legal expression: Article 319 should not be paraphrased as a ban on every conceivable constitutional, elective or public office. The nature of the proposed office matters. Equally, the absence of an Article 319 prohibition does not override other disqualifications, professional rules or conflict-of-interest duties.

💰 Finance & 📊 reporting in Madhya Pradesh

💰 Charged, but still accountable

MPPSC’s expenses are charged on the State Consolidated Fund. They are not put to the Assembly’s vote as a demand for grant, but may be discussed and must remain within the budget, appropriation and financial-rule framework.

Charged ≠ automatically released, unlimited or unaudited.

📊 Report plus reasons

The annual report goes to the Governor. It is laid before the State Legislature together with a memorandum explaining relevant cases in which the Commission’s advice was not accepted.

Article 323 does not prescribe a universal July–August submission date.

🏛️ Act VIII · The Judicial Guardrails

Independent selection. Constitutionally reviewable decisions.

The courts protect institutional independence without treating recruitment as a law-free zone. Separate three questions: was the Commission properly constituted; was the selection fair; and was the final governmental decision lawful? A judgment on one question does not automatically answer the others.

⚖️ Essential case matrix: issue → principle → careful use
CaseIssueCore principleUse carefully
State of U.P. v. Manbodhan Lal Srivastava · 1957 Article 320(3)(c) consultation. The consultation requirement was treated as directory in the relevant sense; non-consultation did not by itself invalidate the disciplinary action. Not permission to disregard statutory rules, fair procedure or Article 323 accountability.
Ashok Kumar Yadav v. State of Haryana · 1985 Selection, interviews and relationships involving selection-body members. Fairness and avoidance of a reasonable apprehension of bias are essential. A member related to a candidate must not participate in interviewing that candidate. Read the institutional context; do not infer that every selection is automatically void.
Shankarsan Dash v. Union of India · 1991 Rights of a candidate on a select list. Selection ordinarily creates no indefeasible right to appointment; the State nevertheless cannot act arbitrarily. A general public-employment principle, not a clause-specific PSC judgment.
Union of India v. S. K. Kapoor · 2011 UPSC advice in disciplinary proceedings. Where advice is relied upon against the employee, natural justice requires a meaningful opportunity to respond to it. Read with the applicable disciplinary rules; non-binding advice can still affect fair-hearing duties.
State of Punjab v. Salil Sabhlok · 2013 Appointment of a State PSC Chairman. Constitutional responsibility demands attention to integrity, competence and institutional purpose; the appointment process is not beyond judicial review. Judicial review does not make courts the ordinary appointing authority or create a universal new qualification.
Tej Prakash Pathak v. Rajasthan High Court · 2024 Changes to recruitment criteria during a selection process. Recruitment must follow the governing rules and Article 14. Mid-process changes to notified eligibility or selection conditions face strict legal constraints. Check whether the governing rules or advertisement permit the change and whether it is non-arbitrary; avoid an exceptionless slogan.

🧩 Four recurring recruitment disputes

🪞

Bias & conflict of interest

Actual dishonesty is not the only concern. Participation, relationships, access to confidential material and the reasonable apprehension of bias may matter. Recusal must be effective, documented and appropriate to the process.

🧾

Eligibility & prescribed dates

Qualifications, age, experience and certificates must be assessed against the governing rules and their cut-off dates. A mandatory rule cannot be casually waived.

📝

Evaluation & expert assessment

Courts ordinarily respect expert academic and selection judgments rather than re-marking answers. Intervention depends on recognised grounds such as rule violation, demonstrable error, mala fides or an arbitrary process.

📣

Transparency & disclosure

Disclosure involves the RTI framework, privacy, examination integrity and the stage of recruitment. “Everything is secret” and “everything must be disclosed immediately” are both unreliable absolutes.

🔭 Act IX · The Institution Tomorrow

Protect the examination. Improve the institution.

A sound reform agenda preserves impartiality while improving speed, accessibility and public trust. Distinguish existing law, documented practice and proposals. A futuristic interface is not evidence that a particular recruitment has already adopted online prelims, remote interviews or AI evaluation.

🧭

Appointments with institutional purpose

Transparent consideration of integrity, competence, experience and conflicts can strengthen confidence in PSC leadership. A structured selection process and a reasoned institutional record are reform ideas consistent with constitutional responsibility.

Do not present a proposed search committee or public hearing as an existing requirement of Article 316.

⏱️

Predictable recruitment calendars

Better vacancy planning, timely requisitions, realistic schedules and stage-wise updates reduce uncertainty. Departments must supply clear rules and vacancy data; delay is not always attributable to the Commission alone.

🔐

Examination integrity

Secure question-setting, controlled printing and transport, vendor accountability, access logs, incident response and protection against impersonation are essential. Technology should support auditable safeguards, not merely create an appearance of security.

♿

Accessible and inclusive assessment

Reasonable accommodation, accessible centres, clear scribe rules, usable digital forms and lawful language options turn formal equality into real opportunity. Apply the disability-rights framework and current examination instructions.

🧪

Assessment quality

Questions should test the competencies a service requires. Clear rubrics, trained evaluators, careful moderation and validated interview methods improve consistency without reducing selection to an opaque algorithm.

📊

Better public reporting

Annual reports can make cycle times, vacancies, accessibility measures, litigation trends and advice deviations easier to understand. Publish defined datasets with the year, denominator and methodology attached.

🛡️ Public Examinations Act, 2024: the correct connection

The Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024 creates a statutory framework against specified unfair means and organised malpractice in the public examinations it covers. UPSC is among the authorities listed in its Schedule. Its coverage is not the same as automatic application to every State examination merely because that examination is public.

📜 Legal precision: read the Act, its Rules, commencement notifications and Schedule. For an MPPSC examination, separately identify the applicable State and other relevant laws; do not assume the central Act supplies the entire State examination framework.

✍️ Mains lens: independence without isolation

✅ What deserves protection

Security of tenure, fair assessment, necessary confidentiality, reasoned professional advice and insulation from patronage. Lawful reservation is not the opposite of merit; recruitment operates within the equality framework.

🔎 What deserves scrutiny

Unexplained delays, conflicts of interest, poor communication, opaque procurement, inaccessible procedures and arbitrary departures from notified rules. Independence is a reason for higher institutional standards — not immunity from them.

🎯 Scenario cards: decide before revealing

🌳 An MPPSC member is accused of misconduct. Can the Governor remove the member?

No. Removal is by the President under Article 317. For misbehaviour, the Supreme Court inquiry and a report that removal is warranted are required. After the Presidential reference, the Governor may suspend under 317(2).

🔐 A former MPPSC member is offered the Chairmanship of MPPSC itself. Is it barred?

Not by Article 319(d). It expressly permits Chairmanship of that or another State PSC. This is a different office, not ordinary reappointment as a member.

💰 The Assembly does not vote on MPPSC’s charged expenses. Are appropriation and audit unnecessary?

No. Charged status removes the vote on that expenditure as a demand for grant; it does not remove the budget, appropriation, financial-rule or audit framework.

📝 A candidate appears on a PSC select list. Has the PSC appointed the candidate?

No. Recommendation and appointment are distinct. The competent authority appoints, subject to the governing rules and non-arbitrariness.

🤝 In a bicameral State, the Assembly alone approves a Joint PSC proposal. Is the Article 315 process complete?

No. Each House of each participating State Legislature must pass the resolution. Parliament may then provide for the Joint Commission by law.

💬 An officer says every disciplinary order made without PSC consultation is automatically void. Correct?

No. Check the consultation framework, exemptions, governing rules and natural justice. Manbodhan Lal rejects automatic invalidity on the bare constitutional non-consultation argument.

📋 A government wants to reduce an existing member’s service benefits to express displeasure. Permissible?

No. Article 318 protects against disadvantageous variation after appointment. The safeguard prevents indirect pressure through worsened service conditions.

🌐 An executive announcement says every municipal post is now recruited through the PSC. Is Article 321 satisfied?

Not by that announcement. Article 321 requires an Act of the competent Legislature to confer the additional functions. Check whether another applicable law already supplies the authority.

⚡ Rapid recall: the precision round

1. Which Part and Chapter contain the PSC provisions?

Part XIV, Chapter II, Articles 315–323. The Part is titled “Services under the Union and the States”.

2. What is the exact composition expression in Article 316?

“As nearly as may be one-half”. Those members must have held government office for at least ten years at their respective appointment dates.

3. Is a fixed membership strength stated in the Constitution?

No. Article 318 permits regulations determining the number of members and staff.

4. Is reappointment to the same PSC office ordinarily permitted?

No. Article 316(3) bars reappointment to that office. Specified different-office routes appear in Article 319.

5. Must an Acting Chairman be the senior-most member?

No constitutional rule says so. Under 316(1A), the appropriate authority appoints one of the other members to perform the duties.

6. Which three Article 317(3) grounds avoid the 317(1) inquiry route?

Adjudged insolvency, outside paid employment during tenure, and infirmity of mind or body. The President still makes the removal order.

7. Where is government-contract interest treated as deemed misbehaviour?

Article 317(4), with its incorporated-company exception.

8. Can an SPSC Chairman later become an ordinary UPSC member?

Yes. Article 319(b) permits UPSC Chairmanship or membership and Chairmanship of another State PSC.

9. Can a UPSC member become an ordinary SPSC member under Article 319(c)?

No. The named routes are UPSC Chairman or State PSC Chairman.

10. Distinguish Article 315(4) from Article 320(2).

315(4): the Governor requests UPSC service for a State, with Presidential approval. 320(2): UPSC assists two or more requesting States with joint recruitment schemes for services requiring special qualifications.

11. What are Article 320’s five advisory heads?

Methods; principles and suitability; discipline; legal-defence cost claims; injury-pension claims. The article also covers matters referred to the Commission.

12. What does Article 320(4) expressly exclude from consultation?

The manner of making provision under Article 16(4) and giving effect to Article 335.

13. What is the minimum laying period in Article 320(5)?

Not less than fourteen days for exemption regulations, subject to modification or repeal by the legislature.

14. Does Article 321 itself cover every public institution automatically?

No. The additional function must be provided for by the competent Legislature’s Act.

15. What accompanies an Article 323 annual report?

A memorandum explaining the reasons for non-acceptance in the relevant cases where the Commission’s advice was not accepted.

16. Is UPSC the administrative superior of all State PSCs?

No. State PSCs are not UPSC regional offices, and the Joint Commission is not an appellate tier.

17. Which 1976 amendment raised the State/Joint PSC retirement age?

The 41st Constitutional Amendment raised it from 60 to 62. UPSC’s ceiling is 65.

18. Which “IFS” is an All India Service?

The Indian Forest Service. The Indian Foreign Service is a Central service.

19. Is a Third Schedule oath prescribed for PSC members?

No. Do not invent a constitutional oath for these offices.

20. What are MPPSC’s core historical coordinates?

1 November 1956; headquarters at Indore. Verify current office-holders, strength, addresses and statistics from official records.

📚 A compact constitutional glossary

🏛️ Constitutional body
An institution whose essential existence or framework is provided by the Constitution; its actual powers still depend on the relevant provisions.
📜 Statutory body
An institution created by legislation. A Joint State PSC is created by Parliamentary law under the enabling constitutional provision.
💬 Consultation
Seeking and considering the relevant expert advice; not the same as requiring agreement or concurrence.
🖋️ Appointment
The legally effective act of the competent appointing authority; not identical to a Commission’s recommendation.
🔐 Reappointment
A fresh appointment to the same office; distinguish it from appointment to a different PSC office expressly permitted by Article 319.
🛑 Suspension
Temporary exclusion from office under the applicable legal power; it is not removal or a final finding of wrongdoing.
💰 Charged expenditure
Expenditure not put to the legislative vote as a demand for grant; budgeting, appropriation and scrutiny still matter.
📝 Memorandum
Under Article 323, the explanation accompanying the annual report concerning relevant non-acceptance of Commission advice.
⚖️ Judicial review
Judicial examination of legality, constitutional compliance and recognised review grounds — not routine substitution of the court’s preferred candidate.
🧭 Directory provision
A provision whose breach does not necessarily carry automatic invalidity in the relevant context; it does not simply mean “optional”.
